Understand tuition fees, hostel expenses, training costs and other expenses before choosing a Merchant Navy course.

Example Course Fee Information

💰 Merchant Navy Course Fee Guide

Merchant Navy courses ki fees institute, course aur duration ke according vary karti hai.

  • DNS: approximately ₹2–4 lakh
  • B.Sc Nautical Science: approximately ₹3–6 lakh
  • Marine Engineering: approximately ₹4–8 lakh
  • GP Rating: approximately ₹1–2 lakh

What Does Your Total Cost Include?

Course fee is not always the complete amount you will spend.

🎓 Tuition Fee

The main academic or training fee charged by the institute for the programme.

🏠 Hostel & Mess

Residential students may have additional accommodation and food expenses.

👕 Uniform & Equipment

Uniforms, books, safety equipment and other required items may cost extra.

🩺 Medical

Medical examination and related certification expenses may apply.

📚 Training & Exams

Examination, practical training and maritime certification costs may be charged separately.

🧾 Other Charges

Registration, documentation, travel and other institute-specific charges may also apply.

Important Before Paying Fees

Always verify the complete fee structure before admission.

✅ Ask for the Complete Fee Structure

Do not rely only on a fee mentioned in advertisements. Ask the institute for the complete written fee structure, including tuition, hostel, mess, training, examination, uniform and other applicable charges.


✅ Check Institute Approval

For maritime training, students should verify the relevant institute and course approval through official sources before paying admission or training fees.


⚠️ Do Not Assume Every College Has the Same Fee

Fees can differ significantly between universities, IMU campuses and affiliated or private institutes. Always check the current fee for the exact institute and academic year.


💡 Keep Extra Budget

Students should also plan for travel, medical examinations, documentation, uniforms, books, equipment and other personal expenses.
