Before Choosing a College

Don't choose an institute only because of advertisements or promises about salary.

✅ Check DGS Approval

Always verify the current approval status of the relevant institute and course through official maritime authorities before paying any admission or training fee.

📋 Check the Exact Course

A college may offer several maritime programmes. Check the approval and eligibility for the specific course you want to join.

💰 Check the Complete Fees

Compare the complete cost, including tuition, hostel, examination, medical, uniform, equipment and other applicable charges.

🩺 Check Medical Requirements

Merchant Navy programmes can have specific medical and eyesight requirements. Verify the current requirements before admission.

⚠️ Important Warning for Students

Never pay money to an agent or institute based only on a promise of guaranteed placement, guaranteed salary or guaranteed employment.

Always verify the institute, course, approval, eligibility and current admission information from official sources before making a financial decision.
