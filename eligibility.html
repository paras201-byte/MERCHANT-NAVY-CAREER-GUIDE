Merchant Navy Eligibility

Understand the basic educational, age, medical and other requirements for starting a Merchant Navy career in India.

Basic Eligibility Requirements

Requirements depend on the course and institute.

🎓 Educational Qualification

Different Merchant Navy courses have different educational requirements.

  • Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics may be required for several officer-entry programmes.
  • English proficiency is generally important.
  • Some rating courses have different academic requirements.

🎂 Age Requirement

Age limits vary according to the course, institute and admission rules.

  • Check the latest age requirement before applying.
  • Course-specific rules may apply.
  • Always verify current requirements from the official institute.

🏥 Medical Fitness

Medical fitness is an important requirement for working at sea.

  • Medical examination is generally required.
  • Eyesight standards may apply.
  • Hearing and overall physical fitness may be assessed.

Eligibility by Course

Common Merchant Navy pathways have different entry requirements.

⚓ DNS

Diploma in Nautical Science is a deck-side training pathway.

  • Academic requirements apply.
  • Medical fitness is required.
  • Age and eyesight requirements may apply.

🎓 B.Sc Nautical Science

A degree-level programme for students interested in the deck department.

  • Science subjects may be required.
  • Medical standards apply.
  • Institute-specific admission rules should be checked.

⚙️ Marine Engineering

A technical pathway leading towards marine engineering careers.

  • Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics requirements may apply.
  • Medical fitness is required.
  • Check the current institute eligibility criteria.

🚢 GP Rating

A rating-level maritime training pathway.

  • Educational requirements vary.
  • Medical fitness is important.
  • Age criteria depend on the applicable rules.
⚠️ Important: Eligibility rules can change and may differ between courses and institutes. Always verify the latest requirements from the official institute and applicable maritime authorities before admission.
