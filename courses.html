Merchant Navy Courses in India

Understand the major education and training pathways available for students interested in a Merchant Navy career.

Popular Merchant Navy Courses

Learn what each pathway is generally designed for.

🚢 DNS

Diploma in Nautical Science is a nautical training pathway associated with the deck department.

  • Focus: Deck / Nautical side
  • Leads towards officer-level deck career progression
  • Admission and sponsorship requirements can vary
  • Check current IMU and DGS requirements before applying

🎓 B.Sc Nautical Science

A degree-level nautical science pathway for students interested in building a career in the deck department.

  • Focus: Navigation and nautical studies
  • Includes academic and practical training
  • Career path is connected with deck operations
  • Eligibility should be checked for the current admission year

⚙️ Marine Engineering

A technical pathway focused on ship machinery, propulsion systems and marine engineering operations.

  • Focus: Engine / technical department
  • Engineering-based education and training
  • Involves machinery and technical systems
  • Exact course eligibility varies by programme

⚓ Electro-Technical Officer (ETO)

A specialized Merchant Navy course for students interested in electrical and electronic systems on ships.

  • Focus on electrical and electronic ship systems
  • Practical technical training
  • Career as an Electro-Technical Officer
  • Eligibility depends on the current admission requirements

🔌 ETO

Electro-Technical Officer training is focused on electrical and electronic systems used on ships.

  • Focus: Electrical and electronic systems
  • Suitable academic background may be required
  • Includes maritime and technical training
  • Check DGS-approved course requirements

🛠️ GME

Graduate Marine Engineering is a pathway for eligible engineering graduates who want to enter the marine engineering field.

  • Focus: Marine engineering
  • Designed for eligible engineering graduates
  • Includes marine-specific training
  • Current eligibility should be verified before admission

🧭 GP Rating

General Purpose Rating is a pre-sea training pathway for eligible candidates entering rating-level maritime roles.

  • Deck and engine-related rating duties
  • Pre-sea training pathway
  • Academic and age requirements apply
  • Always verify current DGS requirements

Before Choosing a Course

Important things every student should check.

✅ Check Institute Approval

Before paying any admission or training fee, students should verify whether the relevant institute and course have the required approval from the Directorate General of Shipping (DGS).


📋 Check Current Eligibility

Age, academic qualifications, medical standards and other requirements can depend on the course and current admission rules. Always check the latest official requirements before applying.


💰 Check the Complete Cost

Do not look only at tuition fees. Students should also check hostel, examination, medical, uniform, equipment, training and other applicable charges.
