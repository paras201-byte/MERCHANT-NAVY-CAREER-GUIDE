Merchant Navy Career & Salary

Complete guide to building a career in the Merchant Navy

⚓ Merchant Navy Career

Merchant Navy is a professional maritime career where seafarers work on commercial ships that transport cargo and goods around the world.

A career in the Merchant Navy can provide international exposure, professional growth and attractive salary opportunities.

🚢 Main Career Departments

⚓ Deck Department

Deck officers are responsible for navigation, cargo operations, ship safety and overall deck operations.

  • Deck Cadet
  • Third Officer
  • Second Officer
  • Chief Officer
  • Captain / Master

🔧 Engine Department

Marine engineers operate and maintain the ship's engines, machinery and technical systems.

  • Marine Engineering Cadet
  • Fourth Engineer
  • Third Engineer
  • Second Engineer
  • Chief Engineer

🛠️ Rating Department

Ratings support deck and engine officers in daily ship operations, maintenance and safety activities.

  • GP Rating
  • Deck Rating
  • Engine Rating
  • AB Seaman

💰 Merchant Navy Salary

Merchant Navy salary depends on rank, experience, company, vessel type and contract.

Deck Cadet: Entry-level salary varies by company and contract.
Third / Second Officer: Salary generally increases with rank and experience.
Chief Officer: Experienced senior deck officer with higher earning potential.
Captain: Senior-most deck rank with significant responsibility and earning potential.
Marine Engineer: Salary increases significantly with experience and rank.

⚠️ Salary figures can change depending on company, vessel, rank, contract and experience. Always verify current salary information before making a career decision.

🎯 Is Merchant Navy a Good Career?

Merchant Navy can be a good career option for students who are interested in ships, travel, technical work and a disciplined professional environment.

  • International work environment
  • Career growth opportunities
  • Different deck and engine career paths
  • Professional maritime training
  • Opportunity to work with international shipping companies
