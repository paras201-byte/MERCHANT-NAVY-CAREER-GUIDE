About Merchant Navy Career Guide

Your Guide to Courses, Colleges, Fees & Careers in Merchant Navy

Who We Are

Merchant Navy Career Guide is an informational website created to help students understand career opportunities in the Merchant Navy.

Choosing a Merchant Navy career can be confusing because students need to understand courses, eligibility requirements, colleges, fees, medical standards and the admission process.

Our goal is to bring these topics together in one easy-to-understand place so that students can research their options more easily.

💰 Fees

📋 Eligibility

📝 Admission Process

Our Mission

Our mission is to make Merchant Navy career information easier for students to understand.

We aim to explain complicated information in simple language and encourage students to verify important admission, fee, medical and eligibility details with the relevant official authorities and institutes.

Important Information

Please verify important information before making any admission or financial decision.

Course eligibility, fees, admission dates, medical standards, sponsorship requirements and institute rules can change. Students should check the latest information from official sources such as the Indian Maritime University (IMU), Directorate General of Shipping (DGS), and the concerned institute.

Why This Website?

Many students search online for Merchant Navy information but find scattered or outdated information.

Merchant Navy Career Guide aims to organize the major topics in a simple format so students can use the website as a starting point for their research.

We are continuously working to improve the website and add more useful information for aspiring Merchant Navy students.
