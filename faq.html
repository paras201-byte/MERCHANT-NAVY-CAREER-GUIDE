Merchant Navy FAQ

Answers to common questions about Merchant Navy courses, eligibility, admission, fees, salary and career options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Starting a Merchant Navy career can raise many questions. Here are some common questions students ask before choosing a course or institute.

1. What is the Merchant Navy?

Merchant Navy refers to the commercial shipping sector. Seafarers work on commercial vessels that transport cargo, containers, oil, gas and other goods.

2. Is Merchant Navy the same as Indian Navy?

No. Merchant Navy and Indian Navy are different. Merchant Navy mainly operates commercial ships, while the Indian Navy is India's naval defence service.

3. Which courses are available for Merchant Navy?

Some common routes include B.Sc Nautical Science, Diploma in Nautical Science (DNS), B.Tech Marine Engineering, ETO-related programmes and other maritime training courses.

4. What is the eligibility for Merchant Navy courses?

Eligibility depends on the specific course and current admission rules. Requirements can include educational qualifications, subjects such as Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, English marks, age limits and medical fitness.

Always check the latest requirements for the exact course and institute before applying.

5. Is medical fitness required for Merchant Navy?

Yes. Medical fitness is an important part of entering seafaring careers. The applicable medical standards depend on the course and seafarer role.

Students should use authorised medical examination information and verify the current requirements before admission.

6. How much does Merchant Navy training cost?

Fees vary depending on the course, institute, accommodation, facilities and other charges.

Students should check the latest fee structure directly with the institute before making any payment.

7. How much salary can a Merchant Navy officer earn?

Salary varies according to rank, experience, vessel type, shipping company, contract terms and other factors.

Because salaries can change, students should not rely on one fixed salary figure advertised online.

8. Which is better: Deck or Engine?

Deck and Engine are different career paths and involve different responsibilities.

Deck careers focus heavily on navigation, cargo and ship operations, while engine careers focus on machinery, propulsion and technical systems.

The suitable choice depends on your interests, educational background and the type of work you want to perform.

9. What is DNS?

DNS stands for Diploma in Nautical Science. It is a training route associated with the deck officer career path, subject to applicable eligibility, sponsorship, medical and regulatory requirements.

10. What is B.Sc Nautical Science?

B.Sc Nautical Science is a degree programme associated with the deck department and navigation career path.

11. What is Marine Engineering?

Marine Engineering is a technical field focused on the operation and maintenance of ship engines, machinery, propulsion systems and other technical equipment.

12. What does an ETO do?

ETO stands for Electro-Technical Officer. ETOs work with electrical, electronic, automation and control systems on ships.

13. Is sponsorship required for Merchant Navy admission?

This depends on the course and admission route. For example, certain DNS admissions in affiliated institutes may have specific sponsorship requirements.

Always check the latest official admission notice for your intended course.

14. How can I check whether an institute is approved?

Students should verify the institute and course using current information from the relevant maritime authorities and official university or institute sources.

15. Can Merchant Navy students work on international ships?

Merchant ships can operate on international routes, but actual employment and vessel assignment depend on the employer, vessel, contract and applicable qualifications.

16. How long can a seafarer stay away from home?

Contract duration and time at sea can vary depending on the company, vessel, route and employment agreement. Students should understand this aspect of the career before joining.

17. Can I join Merchant Navy after 12th?

Yes, several Merchant Navy training and degree routes are available after 10+2, subject to the eligibility requirements of the specific programme.

18. Should I choose a college only because it promises a high salary?

No. Students should verify course approval, eligibility, training facilities, placement information, fees and other important details before choosing an institute.

19. Are Merchant Navy jobs guaranteed after completing a course?

Students should be careful with advertisements promising guaranteed jobs. Employment depends on qualifications, training, certification, company requirements and available opportunities.

20. Where can I find official Merchant Navy information?

Important information can be checked through Indian Maritime University and the Directorate General of Shipping, along with the official website of the relevant institute.

⚠️ Important Before Admission

Admission rules, fees, course requirements and dates can change. Before paying fees or submitting documents, always verify the latest information from the official university, institute and maritime authority.
