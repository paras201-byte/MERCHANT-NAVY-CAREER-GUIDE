Important information about the content provided on this website

General Information

Merchant Navy Career Guide provides educational and informational content related to Merchant Navy courses, colleges, fees, eligibility, admission procedures, careers and related topics.

The information published on this website is intended to help students and aspiring Merchant Navy candidates understand the available options and basic admission requirements.

Information May Change

Course fees, eligibility requirements, admission dates, examination procedures, medical requirements, college details and other information may change from time to time.

Users should always verify the latest information with the relevant official authority, university, institute or government department before making an admission or financial decision.

Official Sources

Wherever possible, users should verify important information through official sources such as:

  • Indian Maritime University (IMU)
  • Directorate General of Shipping (DGS)
  • Official college or institute websites
  • Official admission portals and notifications

Fees and Financial Information

Any fee figures shown on this website are provided for informational purposes and may not represent the current fee charged by a particular institute.

Students should confirm the latest tuition fees, hostel fees, examination fees, medical costs, deposits and other charges directly with the concerned institute.

Inclusion of a college, course or institute on this website does not mean that Merchant Navy Career Guide guarantees its admission, placement, sponsorship, employment or any specific career outcome.

Career and Salary Information

Career opportunities, salaries, promotions, employment conditions and benefits can vary depending on the employer, vessel, rank, experience, qualifications, market conditions and other factors.

Salary information should therefore be treated as general guidance rather than a guaranteed income.
